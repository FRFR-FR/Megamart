# Britain's Secret Underground Mail Railway

Britain once ran a railway underneath London that most people never saw.

The Post Office built the Mail Rail to move letters beneath the streets, away from traffic and crowds.

At its busiest, electric trains rushed through the tunnels carrying mail between sorting stations.

Look at the workers here. Parcels were loaded, pushed onto trains, sorted, and sent straight back underground.

And the strangest part? The railway was tiny. Some tunnels were barely wide enough for the little trains.

For decades, it quietly carried millions of letters while London carried on above it.

Then road transport and changing mail volumes made the system less useful.

So Britain eventually shut the hidden railway down.

A whole railway under London — and almost nobody knew it was there.
