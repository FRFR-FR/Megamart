# Denge Sound Mirrors — crowded scene and production notes

## Visual direction
A working 1930s RAF acoustic-test range, not an empty landscape. Preserve the package’s existing cartoon-3D design, articulated character rig, native camera system, lens values, transitions, lighting presets and 13-shot schedule. No renderer changes or replacement camera language.

## Scene density and activity
- 29 individually rigged people across the opening listening crew, slab crew, dish operators, sound-wall technicians and second listening station
- Engineers and ground crew in distinct period outfits, with walking paths, raised-arm signals, pointing gestures and changing expressions
- Two moving service vehicles on the rear coastal access lane
- A timed 1930s biplane flyover during the wide reveal, assembled from timed standard box props
- Two wind-animated Union Jack signal flags, shoreline boat, moving clouds, bicycle, field huts and crew rest/communications areas
- Equipment cases, instrument tables, chairs, barriers, marker cones, antenna masts and an aerial cable around the three mirror installations

All added scenery uses existing repository prop builders and existing actor keyframes. The original 13 camera-shot entries remain unchanged. No new camera shots, camera settings, character rig changes, generated art or imported stock assets.

## Shot-to-narration intent
- 0.0–2.4s: front-facing lead operator, a visible crew clustered behind him and field activity in the background
- 2.4–6.3s: populated test range, three mirror designs, moving service traffic and the biplane crossing overhead
- 6.3–9.5s: the slab, bowl and long wall, with crews and equipment at each installation
- 9.5–12.4s: operator and trumpet/stethoscope demonstration, with technicians working behind him
- 12.4–15.5s: sea-facing view, shoreline boat and distant listening direction
- 15.5–17.9s: two listening teams at separate coastal stations
- 17.9–23.0s: close detail on the curved concrete face and sound-focusing explanation
- 23.0–26.6s: operator demonstrates cupping his ears while the populated site remains behind him
- 26.6–28.8s: the radar replacement as the acoustic system becomes obsolete
- 28.8–30.0s: populated wide closing view of the complete test range

## Technical constraints
Output remains 540x960 at 30fps for 30 seconds. Actors remain grounded through the existing kinematic story setting. The original 13 shots are preserved unchanged. Automated render, physics and duration QA must pass before the video is treated as complete.
