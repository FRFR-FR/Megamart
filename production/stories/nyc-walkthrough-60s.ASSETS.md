# NYC Walkthrough — asset and fact notes

## Original story and narration
The script in `nyc-walkthrough-60s.md` is original. Its street-grid detail is grounded in the New York City Municipal Archives' Randel 1811 Manhattan street map and the NYC Landmarks Preservation Commission's historical material on Broadway and the Manhattan grid.

## Visuals
All scene geometry and characters are built by the repository's Cartoon Short Factory renderer using its bundled Three.js / cannon-es scene and prop library. No external footage, copied scripts, or generated-image/video assets are used.

## Ambient sound
The production workflow attempts to fetch **Street Corner 5-30** from Soundimage.org:
https://soundimage.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Street-Corner_5_30.mp3

Creator/credit: Eric Matyas, Soundimage.org — Urban Ambience, Street Corner 5-30. The sound is mixed quietly beneath the original narrator. The creator's requested attribution is preserved here and should remain with this project if the audio is redistributed.

If the external sound download is unavailable during a run, the workflow keeps the narration track and marks the ambient-bed fallback in the run log rather than failing the entire render.
